Design Technologist

Bridging Design Systems and Front-End Development

Core Responsibilities
Translate design system components, tokens, and patterns into implementation-ready specifications for engineering teams.
Act as the primary bridge between Product Design and Front-End Engineering throughout the design-to-development process.
Define component behavior, states, interactions, responsiveness, and accessibility requirements to ensure accurate implementation.
Collaborate with engineers to align Figma components with coded component libraries and Storybook documentation.
Conduct design QA and implementation reviews to ensure pixel-perfect execution and consistency across platforms.
Create and maintain documentation, usage guidelines, and handoff standards for design system components.
Audit existing components and workflows, identifying opportunities to improve scalability, maintainability, and development efficiency.
Partner with Design System and Product teams to establish best practices for design tokens, variables, theming, and component architecture.
Leverage AI-assisted workflows and automation tools to streamline design documentation, handoff, and system maintenance processes.
Required Skills
Advanced knowledge of Figma, Design Systems, Variables, and Component Architecture.
Strong understanding of HTML, CSS, Responsive Design, and modern Front-End frameworks (React, Vue, etc.).
Experience working with Storybook, component libraries, and design token systems.
Ability to communicate technical implementation details to both designers and engineers.
Understanding of accessibility standards and scalable UI architecture.
Familiarity with AI-powered design and development workflows.
